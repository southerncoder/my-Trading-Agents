How to read a card

  • The line is a price scale. The dot is today's price. The flag is the trigger: the price at which the model would buy.
  • Wants proof means the trigger is above today's price. The model wants to see the stock climb first, and accepts paying a little more for that evidence.
  • Wants a discount means the trigger is below today's price. The model likes the company but thinks the price will dip.
  • Walk away is the price that says the idea was wrong.
  • Typical days turns the distance into time: how many average days of movement the stock needs to get there. Dashed-underlined words explain themselves when tapped.
